Marketplace, payments and returns

Pre-launch information: Rebornly Market is not yet generally available. This page explains the intended marketplace model, not an offer to purchase or a substitute for the seller-specific information required at checkout. Updated 28 October 2026.

What Rebornly offers

Rebornly is a community and collection platform for reborn Doll enthusiasts, collectors, artists and sculptors. Market is a planned member-to-member marketplace for eligible reborn Dolls and kits. Market checkout is not yet generally available and is not initially offered in the United States. No item can currently be purchased through this information page.

Who sells the items

When Market is enabled, a buyer's contract will be with the seller identified for the listing, not with Hartfors AB. Members may sell privately or as businesses, subject to eligibility and applicable consumer and product safety requirements. Moderators review listing information and possession photographs before publication, but this does not authenticate an item or guarantee delivery.

Payments and currencies

Where Market is enabled, buyers will be offered Stripe card checkout and eligible Klarna pay-later options. The seller uses a connected Stripe account and completes payment-provider identity checks. The seller selects the listing currency where permitted; the total price, shipping charges and available payment methods must be displayed before checkout. Market's planned platform fee for sellers is 2% of the item price, capped at USD 30 per order, or 0% for eligible Creator Pro members. Exact conversion and payment provider fees will be disclosed in the applicable transaction flow before launch.

Shipping and delivery

Physical goods will be shipped by the relevant seller under the delivery terms presented with the listing. Delivery territories, shipping costs, estimated dispatch or delivery times, customs duties and available tracking may vary by seller and location. Rebornly is not the carrier. No global delivery time or shipping price is promised by this website.

Returns, withdrawals and refunds

Applicable statutory consumer rights depend on the seller's legal status and the laws governing the sale. A genuinely private sale does not automatically carry a statutory consumer withdrawal right. When a professional seller sells to a consumer, mandatory withdrawal, conformity and refund rights may apply. Separately, a seller may offer a voluntary return policy, such as 14 or 30 days, where eligible. Any specific return period, exceptions, return-address instructions and responsibility for return postage must be communicated before purchase. A voluntary return policy never replaces a statutory right. Contact the seller first for a purchase-related return or refund. Payment disputes may also be available through the payment provider or card issuer. Rebornly does not promise to decide buyer-seller contractual disputes or guarantee every purchase. A planned special Rebornly refund undertaking for certain casting preorders will be separately described if and when that product launches.

Problems and support

For questions about the platform, accessibility, reports, suspected fraud or account issues, contact support@rebornlyapp.com. A seller remains the primary contractual contact for a member-listed item's delivery, conformity or return. Hartfors AB may review rule violations and take platform safety actions without determining legal title to a physical item.
